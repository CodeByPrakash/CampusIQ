 
 
2. Sustainable Facility and Estate Intelligence Dashboard for India 
 
Problem Statement:  
Create an AI-powered decision dashboard for government-run and institution-run facilities across India that 
helps analyze sustainability, safety, and operations challenges such as air quality, waste management, water 
usage, energy consumption, traƯic ﬂow, asset utilization, emissions, and climate resilience. The solution 
should be adaptable to hospitals, colleges, company campuses, public-sector units, industrial estates, 
municipal facilities, and large mixed-use institutional zones. 
Description - Expected Features:  
The dashboard should ingest open, synthetic, or sensor-style data; visualize trends; detect anomalies; 
forecast short-term changes; and generate plain-language recommendations for administrators. Example 
modules may include air-quality monitoring, waste-bin overﬂow prediction, energy-usage optimization,

advisories, adjusting energy schedules, identifying pollution hotspots, optimizing facility maintenance, or 
prioritizing inspection zones. Teams may frame the problem for a government hospital, engineering college, 
municipal campus, industrial estate, company campus, or public-sector manufacturing facility.

Guidelines:

dashboards, geospatial libraries, map APIs, IoT simulators, and GenAI-based insight generation. Teams 
should use public datasets, synthetic IoT feeds, or manually prepared sample data. Include assumptions 
behind forecasts and recommendations. If using environmental, safety, or health-impact data, avoid 
overstating conclusions; present outputs as decision-support insights, not oƯicial measurements. Solutions
